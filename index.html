HOW TO PLAY

🕹️ KEYBOARD CONTROLS

  • ← → or A D : Walk Left / Right
  • SHIFT or Z : Sprint / Faster Run
  • SPACE / W / ↑ : Jump (Hold for higher jump)
  • ESC or P : Pause Game

📱 TOUCH / MOBILE

  • On-screen virtual D-pad buttons: LEFT, RIGHT
  • Action buttons: JUMP and RUN
  • Tap pause button on top-right anytime

⚡ RESPONSIVE MECHANICS

  • Coyote Time: Jump for a split-second even after stepping off ledges!
  • Jump Buffer: Press jump right before landing to execute on touchdown.
  • Variable Jump: Tap lightly for a short hop, or hold down to soar higher.

💎 ITEMS & POWER-UPS

  • Coin (10), Gem (50), Chalice (100), Secret Relic (250)
  • Shield (Blue Orb): Protects from 1 incoming hit
  • Speed Boost (Lightning): 10 seconds of rapid sprint
  • Double Jump (Wings): 12 seconds of mid-air jumps
  • Treasure Magnet (Horseshoe): Pulls collectibles towards Dave!
